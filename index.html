HydroSleep

An offline-first baby and caregiver tracker for Android.

No ads. No sale of family health data. Export anytime.

HydroSleep records sleep, feeding, hydration, diapers, temperature, medication, growth, teeth, vaccinations and milestones for one or more children, alongside the parent's own hydration and wellbeing. Records stay on the device unless you choose to sync them to a private Synology or WebDAV NAS you control, or to export them yourself.
